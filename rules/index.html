Connect Cafe a cozy virtual library cafe

House rules, terms & privacy

Version 2026-11-14. Connect Cafe house policies.

Full details: Privacy policy · Deleting your account

House rules

  • Be kind. Everyone here is trying to get work done.
  • Halls are silent: there is no chat. Desks show a nickname, an avatar and a topic.
  • No harassment, hate, sexual content or spam, in nicknames, topics, request notes, or voice rooms.
  • Don’t share other people’s personal information.
  • Don’t record anyone. Voice rooms are live only.
  • Connect Cafe is for people aged 18 and over.

Using Connect Cafe

  • Sign-up is open to anyone aged 18+. You confirm your age with your date of birth when you sign up.
  • Anyone can send a short request to study together; the other person can accept or say “Not now”. You can hide yourself from requests at any time.
  • You can block someone (you stop seeing each other’s desks and requests, and you are separated from any shared room; they are not told) and report someone to the moderators.
  • Moderators review reports at least once a day, and aim to act on harassment and safety reports within 24 hours. Possible actions are a warning, removing a topic, pausing study rooms, or closing an account.
  • Connect Cafe is free. There are no payments, trials or subscriptions in this version.

Privacy notice

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time link.
  • Your nickname, avatar, lamp colour and settings (rhythm, chime, hide-from-requests).
  • That you confirmed you are 18+, when, and which version of these rules you accepted. We do not store your date of birth.
  • Your study sittings: hall, desk, topic, times, focused minutes, and the optional one-line recap. Only you can see your history.
  • Your usual desk per hall, your block list, the study requests you send or receive, and which study rooms you joined.
  • Reports you file. The person you report can’t see them or who filed them; moderators can.

What other people see

While you’re in a hall: your nickname, avatar, lamp colour, desk number, current topic and whether your timer is on focus or a break. In a study room, the other members see your nickname and avatar, and hear you only while your mic is on.

What we don’t do

  • We never record voice rooms and keep no transcripts.
  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No notifications that nudge you to come back.

Services we use

Accounts and data are stored with Supabase. Voice rooms run through LiveKit. The site is served by our web host.

Your choices

You can change your nickname and settings at any time, and delete your account and all of its data from My study log. More in the privacy policy.

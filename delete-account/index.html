Delete your Hush Hall account

You can delete your Hush Hall account and its data yourself, at any time, in the app or on the website.

How to delete your data

  1. Open My study log & account.
  2. Under Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

What is deleted

Straight away and permanently: your profile and settings, sittings and recaps, desk notes and any share links, timetable, usual desks, walking position, blocks, study requests, study-room memberships, notices, and reports. Nothing is kept afterwards. Voice is never recorded, so there is no audio to delete.
